Control Flow & Decision MakingSlide 9
Debugging mindset

Common beginner mistakes

IAN 601 · Module 2

= vs ==

= assigns a value. == compares two values.

Missing colon

if sales > 800: needs a colon at the end.

Wrong indentation

The lines controlled by if must be indented consistently.

Simple check

Read the code out loud: “If this is true, do this; otherwise, do that.”

Teaching tip: Most early errors are structure errors, not math errors.

Speaker notes

Normalize errors. Students often think a syntax error means they are bad at programming. Remind them that Python is strict about symbols and spacing.

What to listen for

Instructor checklist

IAN 601 · Module 2

Can students read a condition?

They should explain what makes it true or false.

Can students identify the block?

They should point to the indented lines controlled by the statement.

Can students trace a loop?

They should follow one repetition at a time.

Can students avoid infinite loops?

They should identify the changing variable in a while loop.

Can students connect to analytics?

They should describe classify, count, flag, and summarize patterns.

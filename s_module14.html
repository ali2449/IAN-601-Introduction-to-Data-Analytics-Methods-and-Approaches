Responsible modeling

Avoid leakage

IAN 601 · Module 14

Data leakage happens when training uses information that would not be available at prediction time.

  • Split data before final evaluation.
  • Do not let the target leak into features.
  • Clean training data and test data consistently.
  • Report limitations clearly.
